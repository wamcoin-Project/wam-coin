Before anything else

This coin may be worth nothing. The overwhelming majority of new cryptocurrencies fail, and there is no reason to assume this one is different. Nobody is guaranteed to buy it from you. There is no price on this page and there never will be. Treat anything you acquire as money you can afford to lose entirely.

What it is

A fork of Bitcoin Core v28.1 with four deliberate departures: a hard ceiling of 22,000,000 coins, a two-minute block target, RandomX proof of work so that ordinary CPUs stay competitive against purpose-built hardware, and difficulty retargeting on every block so that a rented hash-rate spike cannot freeze the chain.

Roughly 250,000 lines of peer-to-peer networking, script interpretation and wallet code are inherited from a codebase that has been adversarially reviewed for fifteen years. Retyping that would not have made WAM more original — it would have made it less safe. About 3,000 lines are genuinely ours, and every one of them is public.

None of the founder reserve is spendable at launch. All 2,000,000 are locked in the genesis block itself by OP_CHECKLOCKTIMEVERIFY, releasing 20% a year from September 2027 to September 2031. The 5% treasury fee is a consensus rule that stops permanently at block 400,000, after which miners receive the entire subsidy. Neither is a promise; both are enforced by every node, and you can read the lock out of block 0 yourself.

What the founder does not do

The founder keeps the founder reserve and grants himself nothing above it. He does not sell WAM and does not support its price — because a founder who accumulates beyond his own share corrupts the distribution, and a coin concentrated in one hand is not distributed however its schedule reads.

If he ever needs it for something, he buys it the way you would: from a miner or a venue, at whatever it is worth that day.

This is a restriction he placed on himself for the fairness of the distribution. It is not a promise to you, and it puts a floor under nothing.

Do not take our word for it

Every claim above has a command that proves or disproves it. These read the shipped binary, not this page:

python3 scripts/verify_supply.py

# every difference between WAM and Bitcoin, with the reason for each
python3 scripts/patch_upstream.py --list

# what the running chain says about its own money

And one that is not ours to write. On 26 August 2026 SatoshiLabs merged WAM into the registry every wallet reads before it derives an address: SLIP-0044 coin type 5718349, and SLIP-0173 prefix wam. The numbers are reserved to this chain and to no other. That is a fact about us kept by somebody else — it is not an endorsement, a listing, or a promise that any wallet will add support.

It is a reserve name at a different registrar, on a different account, with a different payment method, so that no single company's decision can take both. It was written into CHANNELS.txt and signed before it was needed, because a name that first appears during an outage cannot be told apart from an impostor's. Neither name carries the chain: the seed addresses are compiled into the software rather than looked up by name, and a download proves itself by the signature on SHA256SUMS wherever it is served from.

Nobody speaking for WAM will message you first, offer you an allocation, or ask you for a key or a payment — there is nothing to buy and no reason anyone would need to reach you. CHANNELS.txt is the plain-text copy of this list, kept so it can be signed and checked rather than merely believed.

Mining

WAM is mined by ordinary computers. There is no pre-sale, no allocation to buy into, and no way to acquire it at launch except by mining it or by trading with someone who did.

Mainnet opened 15 September 2026. The testnet still runs for development, and its coins are worth nothing by design.

If you run a node, subscribe to releases on the repository — Watch ▾ → Custom → Releases. Some releases change a consensus rule, and a node left on an old one is rejected by the network at the next block it disagrees about. It does eventually warn you, in a log nobody is reading. A release notification arrives where you got the software, before any of that.

One pool is not a network. There is a project pool at pool.wamcoin.org, and three more run by other people are on the mining page — which is what a network looks like, and what this project cannot manufacture. What keeps a chain decentralised is not how a pool is built — a pool is central by definition — it is that a miner can leave for one somebody else runs, or mine alone. POOL_OPERATOR.md is the whole thing: architecture, configuration, payouts, and what it costs to run.

The founder does not mine, and holds no WAM. Not one unit, from the genesis block to today. The founder reserve in the genesis coinbase is locked by consensus until September 2027 — five outputs behind OP_CHECKLOCKTIMEVERIFY, readable from any node — and he has said in public that he will not spend it when it opens.

This paragraph said the opposite until 4 October 2026. Written before launch, it said he would mine from the public pool, that the addresses he mined to would be published here, and that his share of the hash rate would be large at launch and fall. None of it happened, because he never mined: it was a plan in the future tense, left standing after the future arrived. What secures this chain is the people mining it, and what they are doing is on the explorer rather than described here.

Trading

WAM settles by atomic swap. A swap is two coins moving at once or neither moving, with nobody holding both halves in between.

Enquiries were sent to six decentralised venues before launch — Komodo Wallet, BasicSwap DEX, Bisq, Maya Protocol, Haveno and Block DX — asking what each requires to list a chain like this. One has merged it: WAM entered the Komodo / GLEEC coins registry on 22 September 2026. That repository's maintainer opened the pull request himself, ran a test swap, and merged it. No listing fee was asked for and none was paid; he asked for coin to test a swap with, since nobody can swap a coin he holds none of, so 500 WAM went to him from the treasury — in the treasury ledger with its transaction id — and he swapped 249.68 WAM against Litecoin. The other five are still requests for requirements, with nothing agreed.

A real, organic exchange has happened five times: somebody did work for this project and took WAM as the payment for it. The first was paid 10,000 WAM for a pool accounting fault, and the second 10,000 for another one — the amount the bounty table gave that class of finding at the time — in nine transactions each. The third was paid 1,000 for a fault that could have destroyed a round's payout table. The fourth, 250 for address validation that refused one valid address in twenty. The fifth, 10,250 for a broadcast payment that could go out a second time, and three further faults in the same accounting. Then on 9 November: 900 to that same reviewer for a denial of service against the node and two further faults, 800 to a sixth for the pool's block commit and for a fault he then found in our fix for it, and 250 to a seventh for the block spool. 34,100 WAM in all, with every transaction id in the treasury ledger and every finding written out in full in the security policy. None of them bought it. Each read this code looking for what was wrong with it, and then accepted its coin for the work.

WAM is its own layer 1 rather than a token on someone else's chain, so it settles by atomic swap or a peer-to-peer order book — you swap another coin for it, directly, with no custodian in between. It cannot appear on an automated market maker such as Uniswap without a bridge and a wrapped representation, which is the same reason Monero does not.

ما هي

تفريعة من Bitcoin Core v28.1 بأربعة اختلافات مقصودة: سقف نهائي قدره 22,000,000 وحدة، وبلوك كل دقيقتين، وإثبات عمل RandomX ليبقى المعالج العادي منافساً أمام العتاد المصنوع خصيصاً للتعدين، وإعادة ضبط للصعوبة عند كل بلوك حتى لا تستطيع قوة تعدين مستأجرة أن تُجمّد السلسلة.

نحو 250,000 سطر من شبكة الند للند وتفسير السكربت وإدارة المحفظة موروثة من قاعدة كود خضعت لمراجعة عدائية طوال خمسة عشر عاماً. إعادة كتابتها ما كانت لتجعل WAM أكثر أصالة — كانت لتجعلها أقل أماناً. نحو 3,000 سطر هي ملكنا فعلاً، وكلها منشورة.

التعدين

تُعدَّن WAM بأجهزة الحاسب العادية. لا يوجد بيع مسبق، ولا حصة تُشترى، ولا طريقة للحصول عليها يوم الإطلاق إلا بتعدينها أو بمبادلتها مع من عدّنها.

تُفتح الشبكة الرئيسية في 15 سبتمبر 2026. وحتى ذلك الحين تعمل الشبكة التجريبية، وعملاتها لا تساوي شيئاً بالتصميم.

ومن يُشغّل عقدةً فليشترك في إشعارات الإصدارات على المستودع — Watch ▾ ← Custom ← Releases. فبعض الإصدارات تُغيّر قاعدة إجماع، وعقدةٌ تبقى على القديم ترفضها الشبكة عند أول كتلةٍ تختلف عليها. وهي تُحذّرك في النهاية، في سجلٍّ لا يقرأه أحد. أمّا إشعار الإصدار فيصلك في المكان الذي أخذتَ منه البرنامج، قبل ذلك كله.

ومجمّعٌ واحد ليس شبكة. يوجد مجمّع للمشروع على pool.wamcoin.org، ويجب أن توجد مجمّعاتٌ أخرى ليست لنا. فما يحفظ لامركزية السلسلة ليس تصميم المجمّع — المجمّع مركزيٌّ بطبيعته — بل أن يستطيع المعدّن أن يرحل إلى مجمّعٍ يديره غيرنا، أو أن يعدّن وحده. وPOOL_OPERATOR.md فيه كل شيء: البنية، والإعداد، والدفعات، وما يكلّفه التشغيل. ومن يُنشئ واحداً فليخبرنا، ويُذكَر هنا بجانب مجمّعنا.

المؤسّس لا يعدّن، ولا يملك من WAM وحدةً واحدة — من كتلة التكوين إلى اليوم. واحتياطي المؤسّس في كتلة التكوين مقفولٌ بالإجماع حتى سبتمبر 2027، خمسة مخرَجات خلف OP_CHECKLOCKTIMEVERIFY تُقرأ من أيّ عقدة، وقد قال علناً إنّه لن ينفقه حين يُفتح.

التداول

تُسوّى WAM بالمبادلة الذرّية. والمبادلة الذرّية أن تتحرّك العملتان معاً أو لا تتحرّك واحدةٌ منهما، ولا يمسك أحدٌ الطرفين في ما بين.

أُرسلت استفسارات إلى ستّ منصّات لا مركزية قبل الإطلاق — Komodo Wallet وBasicSwap DEX وBisq وMaya Protocol وHaveno وBlock DX — سؤالاً عمّا تشترطه لإدراج سلسلة كهذه. وواحدة منها أدرجتها: دخلت WAM سجلّ عملات Komodo / GLEEC في 22 سبتمبر 2026. مشرف ذلك المستودع هو من فتح طلب الدمج بنفسه، وأجرى مبادلة اختبارية، ثم دمجه. ولم يُطلب رسم إدراج ولم يُدفع؛ طلب عملةً يختبر بها المبادلة، إذ لا يبادل أحدٌ عملةً لا يملك منها شيئاً، فخرجت إليه 500 WAM من الخزينة — مكتوبةً في دفتر الخزينة برقم معاملتها — وبادل 249.68 WAM بلايتكوين. والخمس الباقية ما زالت طلب متطلّبات لم يُوافَق على شيء فيها.

وقد وقعت المبادلة العضويّة الحقيقيّة خمس مرّات: رجلٌ عمل عملاً لهذا المشروع وأخذ WAM ثمناً له. فالأوّل أُعطي 10,000 WAM على عطبٍ في حسابات المسبح، والثاني 10,000 على عطبٍ آخر فيها — وهو ما كان جدولُ المكافآت يمنحه لهذا الصنف يومَها — في تسع معاملاتٍ لكلٍّ منهما. والثالث 1,000 على عطبٍ كان يمكن أن يمحو جدول دفع جولةٍ كاملة، والرابع 250 على تحقّقٍ من العناوين كان يرفض عنواناً صحيحاً من كلّ عشرين. والخامس 10,250 على دفعةٍ مبثوثةٍ قد تخرج مرّةً ثانية، وثلاثةِ أعطابٍ أخرى في المحاسبة نفسها. ثمّ في ٩ نوفمبر: 900 للخامس نفسِه على حجب خدمةٍ عن العقدة وعطبَين آخرَين، و800 للسادس على عقدة الكتلة في المسبح وعلى عطبٍ وجده بعدها في إصلاحنا لها، و250 للسابع على مخزن الكتل. 34,100 WAM في الجملة، كلّ معرّف معاملةٍ منها في دفتر الخزينة، وكلّ عطبٍ مشروحٌ بتمامه في سياسة الأمان. ولم يشترِها أحدٌ منهم: كلٌّ قرأ هذه الشيفرة باحثاً عن خللٍ فيها، ثمّ قبل عملتها أجراً على عمله.

WAM سلسلة مستقلّة بذاتها لا عملة رمزية على سلسلة غيرها، فتداولها يكون بمبادلة ذرّية أو بدفتر أوامر بين الأطراف — تبادل عملة أخرى بها مباشرةً بلا وسيط يحتفظ بمالك. ولا يمكن إدراجها في منصّة صانع سوق آلي مثل Uniswap بلا جسر ونسخة ملفوفة — وهو نفس سبب غياب مونيرو عنها.
