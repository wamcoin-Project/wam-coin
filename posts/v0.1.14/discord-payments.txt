He reproduced both against this repository's own code on 127.0.0.1. Nothing touched the live pool or the network.

MESSAGE 4 — dang150296. The rung is 250 WAM and it was not paid, because he did not ask for it.

**dang150296 (Urriki1502) found a fault in v0.1.14 and took nothing for it.**

getsupplyinfo named the last block of the old epoch as the next halving, so the countdown reached zero a block early — on mainnet it said 200,000 where the subsidy actually falls at 200,001. Not a consensus or payout fault, but the explorer serves that number and the announcement bot posts "next halving in N blocks" from it, so the wrong figure reached these channels rather than stopping at an RPC nobody calls by hand.

He found it in a second independent review that rebuilt our patched Core from the tag and ran native regtest against it — and that published the list of what he had NOT tested beside what he had. When the fix went in, he read it and found the same arithmetic written out four more times.

He did not price it and no amount was paid. The rung is recorded at 250, and it is his whenever he asks for it.
